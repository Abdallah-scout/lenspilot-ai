# Studio caption style guide (demo fixture)

- One or two sentences, 10-280 characters.
- Lead with the subject, then the light or mood. Example: "Candlelit dining room, warm light pooling on oak tables."
- Present tense, no hashtags, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- Never name guests, staff, or clients.
- Do not mention camera settings in captions.
- Collections: `restaurants`, `interiors`, `featured`.
